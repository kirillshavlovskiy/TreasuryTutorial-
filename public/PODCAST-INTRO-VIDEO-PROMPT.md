# Simple Sigma — Podcast Intro Video Prompt

**Goal (one sentence):** Circling translucent finance formulas assemble into a left-aligned podcast title. No logo.

**Frame:** 1920×1080 · 16:9 · 8–10s · 30fps  
**Upload:** `podcast-intro-card-bright.png` (background only). Do **not** upload logo files.

---

## What to make

1. Dark navy fintech scene (use uploaded plate or matching void).
2. Semi-transparent cyan/slate **formulas circle and orbit** in 3D (hero of the shot).
3. Those same formulas **fly inward and form the title** (not a cut, not a fade from black).
4. Hold the finished title. Soft fade out.

## What NOT to make

- No σ / σₛ / logo card / badge / “Simple Sigma” wordmark
- No centered poster title
- No hard cut to title text

---

| Time | What happens |

| 6–8s | Formulas peel off the orbit, spiral to **left-center**, scramble, then lock into title lines (brand → hero → footnote). |
| 8–10s | Title holds. Extra formulas fade to faint dust at edges. Soft fade. |
